## Chapters

1. [Probability Spaces](/01_probability_space) — Introduces probability spaces, random variables, CDFs, PDFs, survival functions, hazard rates, and the relationships among these representations.
2. [Universal Transformations and Inverse-Transform Sampling](/02_universal_transforms) — Develops quantile functions, inverse-transform sampling, the probability integral transform, and cumulative-hazard time rescaling.
3. [Modeling Wait Time](/03_wait_time) — Models waiting-time distributions through hazard functions, deriving exponential waiting times from constant hazard and characterizing memorylessness.
4. [From Wait Time to Poisson Process](/04_poisson_process) — Builds the Poisson counting process from exponential waiting times and derives its arrival-time, count, increment, and variable-rate properties.
